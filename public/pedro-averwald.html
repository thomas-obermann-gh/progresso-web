Pedro Averwald

Principal Consultant SAP IBP

Principal consultant with more than 25 years of SAP experience, working with SAP since 2000 across the full supply chain planning stack, from SAP APO to SAP IBP. Pedro specializes in end-to-end SAP IBP implementations, with a focus on Response and Supply, and in their integration with ECC and S/4HANA through CPI-DS, Cloud Integration, RTI and ABAP development.

He has hands-on delivery experience across pharmaceuticals, food, machinery, home appliances, automotive, chemicals, healthcare and logistics, including multiple worldwide rollouts.
