Thomas Obermann

Principal Consultant SAP PP and PP/DS

Specialist in SAP production planning and advanced planning with more than 25 years in global supply chain transformation. Thomas takes production planning from strategy to shop floor: he translates business requirements into SAP PP, PP/DS and ePP/DS solutions, designs finite detailed scheduling with optimizer and heuristics, and connects the plan to execution through MES integration.

A recurring focus is the seam between the planning levels: getting S&OP and demand results out of SAP IBP, Kinaxis or APO into a feasible, finite PP/DS schedule. He is typically brought in where a legacy planning tool has to be replaced, or where an existing PP/DS solution is not delivering the schedule the business needs.
