Example references

  • Aerospace and automotive

    Greenfield S/4HANA with SAP PP and ePP/DS

    Led the planning and production stream from blueprint to integration tests and key user training. Finite, optimizer-based scheduling with setup matrices, joint planning of production and laboratory capacity under aerospace regulations, and a clear split of responsibilities between ERP, APS and MES.

    Result: Regulated production planned end to end in S/4HANA. The mandate was extended to the automotive business.

  • Food and beverages

    IBP and ePP/DS as one planning chain

    IBP and ePP/DS had been implemented separately. We corrected the demand handover to the producing plants, moved from classic MRP to requirements planning in PP/DS, and introduced a setup matrix for sequence-optimized scheduling.

    Result: One consistent planning chain, with planners working from alerts and Fiori apps instead of manual reconciliation.

  • Pharmaceuticals

    SAP IBP Response and Supply, order-based

    Migration from SAP APO with blueprint and end-to-end configuration: heuristic supply planning, safety and target stock logic, sales order consumption, deployment, and real-time integration with several ECC and S/4HANA systems.

    Result: Worldwide roll-out, including support and hypercare.

  • Specialty chemicals

    Kinaxis handover into embedded PP/DS

    Designed and implemented the handover of planned orders from Kinaxis into embedded PP/DS, and introduced the new ePP/DS functions to planners and key users in best-practice workshops.

    Result: Planners work from one finite schedule in S/4HANA, based on the Kinaxis supply plan.

  • Machinery

    SAP IBP and ePP/DS for a high-variance product range

    S&OP design with demand and supply reviews, full ePP/DS configuration with variant configuration, and a custom heuristic that schedules assembly against physical space as well as time.

    Result: S&OP connected through to detailed scheduling, with assembly planned finitely against capacity and the variant mix.

  • Food

    SAP IBP S&OP with APO migration

    Demand planning with promotions, driver-based risks and opportunities and machine-learning forecasts, time-series supply optimization with cost integration and scenarios, and new product introduction.
