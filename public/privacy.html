2. Summary

We do not set cookies and do not use analytics or tracking tools. The website does not embed third-party content. Fonts and images are delivered with the website, not loaded from other servers.

3. Hosting and delivery of the website

The website is delivered via Cloudflare. The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the website, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data to deliver the pages and protect them against attacks. This includes your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested, your browser and operating system, and the page you visited before (referrer). According to Cloudflare, this log data is processed for a limited period only. We do not create user profiles from it.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely, quickly and reliably.

If Cloudflare detects a possible threat in an individual case, a security check may appear. Cloudflare may then set a technically necessary cookie that stores the passed check. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Cloudflare may also process data outside the EU, in particular in the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The transfer is therefore based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). We have concluded a data processing agreement with Cloudflare under Art. 28 GDPR.

4. Contact by email or phone

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details (for example your name, contact details and the content of your enquiry) to deal with your request. Providing them is voluntary, but we cannot answer your enquiry without them.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures. In all other cases, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in dealing with enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We use Microsoft 365 for email. The provider is Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited, One Microsoft Place, South County Business Park, Leopardstown, Dublin 18, Ireland. Microsoft processes the data as a processor. A transfer to the USA is possible. Microsoft Corporation is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

We delete your details once they are no longer needed to deal with your enquiry. Statutory retention periods, in particular under commercial and tax law, remain unaffected.

5. Your rights

You have the right at any time to:

  • Access to your data stored by us (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Withdrawal of any consent given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

Right to object

Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

Right to lodge a complaint

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit) in Wiesbaden.

For all requests concerning your rights, an email to the address above is sufficient.

6. Automated decisions

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling.

7. Encryption

The website is delivered exclusively over an encrypted connection (TLS). You can recognize this by “https://” and the lock icon in your browser's address bar.
